# Third-party notices

## EPUB implementation and samples

The optional `:app:ireadview-epub` module contains the project's own system-WebView renderer. It does not bundle or depend on Readium. Readium Kotlin Toolkit tag 3.1.0 (commit d84e4f8faca256f3027965b5ec6aa7867f0bee62) was consulted as an architectural reference; its source is kept outside this repository. No Readium scripts, CSS or Kotlin implementation are included in this module. Jsoup is used to prepare publication markup; its dependency license remains applicable.

The two Demo EPUB books are original repository-authored examples generated by `tools/Generate-EpubSamples.py`; they contain no downloaded third-party publication or image.

The default EPUB showcase, `the-art-of-arm-assembly.epub` (The Art of ARM Assembly), is separately supplied by the user and copied unchanged from their local file. It is not one of the repository-authored examples; its original copyright and license notices remain applicable.
